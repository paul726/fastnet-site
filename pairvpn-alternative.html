What happened to PairVPN.

  • iOS App StoreListing removed. The App Store page returns 404 and Apple's lookup API shows no result in the US or German storefronts. Users on r/PairVPN began posting “Can't find app” on October 20, 2026 and “Removed from App Store” on November 6.
  • MacOfficial download offline. pairvpn.com/download returns 404. The Mac client was only ever distributed from the developer's site.
  • AndroidStill listed, not maintained. The Google Play build is version 1.9.16, last updated September 15, 2023.
  • DeveloperNo announcement. The website's copyright notice ends at 2024 and nothing has been published about the removal.
  • Already installed?It may keep working for now. A copy already on your iPhone can run until an iOS update or a device change breaks it. A reinstall, a new iPhone, or a second device cannot get it back from the App Store.

Already have PairVPN on your Mac? Here's what changes.

PairVPN ran as a server on the iPhone and a client on the Mac, on top of Personal Hotspot or USB tethering. FastNet keeps the same two-device shape and removes the hotspot layer.

The Mac client → FastNet Connect

PairVPN needed Personal Hotspot or USB tethering switched on first, then tunneled on top of it. FastNet Connect talks to the iPhone directly over the USB cable or over a peer-to-peer Wi-Fi link, and captures Mac traffic system-wide, so there is no proxy to configure in each app.

The iPhone server → ProxyDeck

Free on the App Store. Tap Start Mac Bridge and keep the app in the foreground, the same way you kept PairVPN's server screen on.

Pairing codes → nothing to pair

No 6-digit code, no account, no relay server. The two apps find each other locally, and your traffic never touches a server we operate.

What you lose

Remote access to a home or office network, Windows and Android clients, and consoles or TVs connecting through the Mac. FastNet does one thing: this Mac, online through this iPhone. Used PairVPN on a Windows PC? Get an email if FastNet for Windows ships.

An alternative for a specific job.

If you used PairVPN to get a Mac online through an iPhone, that is the job FastNet is built for. It is not a general-purpose VPN and does not try to be.

Before you switch.

Is PairVPN coming back to the App Store?

We don't know. As of November 6, 2026 the developer has posted no announcement, the Android build hasn't been updated since September 2023, and the official Mac download page is offline. The status section above will be updated if the listing returns.

Can I just sideload PairVPN instead?

Some r/PairVPN users suggest it. You need an IPA you trust, a signing workflow such as AltStore or Sideloadly, re-signing every 7 days on a free developer account, and the Mac client still has to come from somewhere now that the official download is gone. It is doable for a tinkerer. It is not a setup most people will keep maintaining.

Is this a complete replacement for PairVPN?

No. FastNet is an alternative for the specific iPhone-to-Mac internet-sharing use case. It does not replace PairVPN's broader platform support or remote home/office network access.

Do I need Personal Hotspot turned on?

No. Over USB, FastNet Connect reaches ProxyDeck through the cable itself. Over nearby Wi-Fi it uses a direct peer-to-peer link between the two devices. Personal Hotspot stays off. Unsure whether your setup fits? Ask us before buying.

Can I use another iPhone app while connected?

Mac Bridge stops when ProxyDeck leaves the foreground. Do not choose this setup if uninterrupted background operation on your iPhone is essential.

Does it guarantee unlimited data or bypass every hotspot restriction?

No. FastNet does not change your mobile plan or guarantee how a carrier accounts for traffic. Review your plan's terms and allowances. Cellular congestion, signal strength, and device limits can still affect performance.

Is the Mac connection free?

No. The free iPhone app is not a free trial of the full two-app setup. Mac Bridge requires a paid FastNet Connect Mac license. Review the requirements above, or ask us about your setup before buying.
