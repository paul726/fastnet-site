A focused Mac + iPhone setup

FastNet Connect + ProxyDeck

  • Purpose: route Mac traffic through a nearby iPhone's cellular connection.
  • Connection: USB cable or nearby peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. No jailbreak required.
  • Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 13+, iPhone with iOS 16+, and ProxyDeck kept in the foreground.
  • Price: $29 one-time Mac license. The iPhone app is free; the complete Mac Bridge setup is not.

Not an Android, Windows, or remote home-network solution. The iPhone app is a foreground proxy and network utility, not an iOS system-wide VPN.

Two apps. Three steps.

  1. Install ProxyDeck on your iPhone.

    Look for ProxyDeck — Network Lab on the App Store. It is free. Its standalone network checks work without a Mac or purchase.

  2. Set up FastNet Connect on your Mac.

    Open the Mac download and setup guide on your Mac, then install and activate FastNet Connect. Use an existing license or buy a Mac license for $29. The Mac installer cannot run on an iPhone.

  3. Start Mac Bridge and connect.

    In ProxyDeck, open Mac Bridge and tap Start Mac Bridge. Keep ProxyDeck in the foreground, then connect from FastNet Connect over USB or nearby peer-to-peer Wi-Fi.

Before you switch.

Is this a complete replacement for PairVPN?

No. FastNet is an alternative for the specific iPhone-to-Mac internet-sharing use case. It does not replace PairVPN's broader platform support or remote home/office network access.

Will it fix a PairVPN connection problem?

Not necessarily. This page does not claim that PairVPN is broken or incompatible with your devices. FastNet is a different setup with its own requirements, not a fix for PairVPN itself. Ask us about your setup before purchasing if you are unsure.

Can I use another iPhone app while connected?

Mac Bridge stops when ProxyDeck leaves the foreground. Do not choose this setup if uninterrupted background operation on your iPhone is essential.

Does it guarantee unlimited data or bypass every hotspot restriction?

No. FastNet does not change your mobile plan or guarantee how a carrier accounts for traffic. Review your plan's terms and allowances. Cellular congestion, signal strength, and device limits can still affect performance.

Is the Mac connection free?

No. The free iPhone app is not a free trial of the full two-app setup. Mac Bridge requires a paid FastNet Connect Mac license. Review the requirements above, or ask us about your setup before buying.
