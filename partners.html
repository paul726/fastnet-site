What you would be recommending.

FastNet Connect is a Mac app that puts the Mac online through an iPhone's cellular connection, over USB or nearby peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, with Personal Hotspot off. It pairs with ProxyDeck, a free iPhone app.

What it does well

For your audience

  • Uses the phone's own data instead of the capped hotspot allowance. On one T-Mobile line: 0.21 Mbps on the throttled hotspot, 306 Mbps through FastNet on the same SIM.
  • One click on the Mac. It captures traffic system-wide, so there is nothing to configure per app.
  • Free to try, then $29 one-time. The Mac app runs without a license for the first 2 hours of connected time, so your audience can check it on their carrier first. No subscription, 30-day refund, no account, and no traffic through our servers.

Limits to tell them about

Speed figures are from the developer's own back-to-back Speedtest runs; your results depend on signal, plan, and device.
