ProxyDeck

Network Lab for iPhone, with an optional FastNet Connect companion for Mac.

Network Lab works entirely on your iPhone and requires no Mac, account, or companion app. It checks DNS, HTTPS, TCP, and the built-in local SOCKS5 path.

Mac Bridge is optional. It works with FastNet Connect on your Mac over USB or nearby peer-to-peer Wi-Fi and only while ProxyDeck remains in the foreground.

Mac Bridge setup

On an iPhone right now? Open fastnet-site.vercel.app on your Mac before downloading. FastNet.pkg is a macOS installer and cannot be installed on iPhone.

  1. Install ProxyDeck from the App Store on your iPhone.
  2. On your Mac, download FastNet Connect, open FastNet.pkg, and complete the installer.
  3. Launch FastNet Connect on your Mac and activate it with your license key.
  4. Open ProxyDeck on the iPhone, select Mac Bridge, and tap Start Mac Bridge.
  5. Keep ProxyDeck in the foreground, then connect from the Mac over USB or nearby peer-to-peer Wi-Fi.
