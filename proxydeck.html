ProxyDeck

The free iPhone app used with FastNet Connect for Mac and Windows.

Looking for FastNet on the App Store? The iPhone app is called ProxyDeck — Network Lab. FastNet Connect is the separate $29 one-time app for Mac and Windows; connecting a computer requires both.

Install ProxyDeck for iPhone — Free

Network Lab works entirely on your iPhone and requires no computer, account, or companion app. It checks DNS, HTTPS, TCP, and the built-in local SOCKS5 path.

Mac Bridge is optional. It works with FastNet Connect on a Mac (over USB or nearby peer-to-peer Wi-Fi) or on a Windows PC (over the iPhone's Wi-Fi network), and only while ProxyDeck remains in the foreground. The mode is called Mac Bridge in the app, and it is the one for Windows PCs too.

Setup on a Mac

On an iPhone right now? Install ProxyDeck above first. Then open fastnet-site.vercel.app/#mac-setup on your computer for the downloads and license instructions. FastNet Connect installs on a Mac or a Windows PC, not on an iPhone.

  1. Install ProxyDeck from the App Store on your iPhone.
  2. On an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13+, follow the FastNet Connect setup guide, open FastNet.pkg, and complete the installer.
  3. Launch FastNet Connect on your Mac and activate it with your existing license key, or purchase a license for $29 one-time. The iPhone app is free; the license is separate and covers Mac and Windows.
  4. Open ProxyDeck on the iPhone, select Mac Bridge, and tap Start Mac Bridge.
  5. Keep ProxyDeck in the foreground, then connect from the Mac over USB or nearby peer-to-peer Wi-Fi.

Setup on a Windows PC

  1. Install ProxyDeck from the App Store on your iPhone.
  2. On a PC with Windows 10 or 11, download FastNet Connect for Windows and run the installer. The Windows page shows each warning Windows displays and what to click.
  3. Turn on Personal Hotspot on the iPhone and join that Wi-Fi network from the PC. If both are already on the same home Wi-Fi, that works as well.
  4. Click Connect in FastNet Connect. Then open ProxyDeck, select Mac Bridge, tap Scan pairing code, and point the iPhone at the code on the PC. The pairing is remembered.
  5. Keep ProxyDeck in the foreground while the PC is connected. The same $29 license works here: purchase one or use the one you already have.
