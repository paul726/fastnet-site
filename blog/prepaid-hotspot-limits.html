Hotspot Limits on Prepaid Plans in 2026: Visible, Cricket, US Mobile and Google Fi

Prepaid plans say “unlimited” in large type and describe the hotspot in small type. We read the plan pages of four prepaid carriers on November 24, 2026 and wrote down what each plan gives the hotspot. Every figure below is from the carrier's own page, linked under each table. Plans change often, so check the link before you switch.

Three kinds of hotspot limit

  • A speed cap. Hotspot data is unlimited, but devices on the hotspot never go faster than a set speed. Visible works this way.
  • A monthly allowance. The hotspot gets a fixed number of gigabytes. Cricket, US Mobile and Google Fi work this way.
  • No hotspot at all. The cheapest tier of several carriers leaves it out, and Personal Hotspot cannot be switched on.

In all three cases the phone's own apps are treated separately and are usually not affected.

The next section shows how to find the number for your own plan.

How to find your own plan's hotspot limit

  1. Look for the Broadband Facts label. US carriers have to publish one for each plan, in the same layout as a nutrition label. It sits on the plan page or at checkout and states the hotspot terms.
  2. Open the carrier's app. Most show hotspot data used and remaining for the current month.
  3. Check the iPhone's own counter. Settings › Cellular, then scroll to Personal Hotspot. It counts from the last time you tapped Reset Statistics, not from your billing date.

What these numbers mean for a laptop

  • 5 Mbps is enough for web pages, mail, chat and one video stream at moderate quality. A 1 GB download takes close to half an hour, and a video call is fine as long as nothing else is using the connection.
  • 15 Mbps is comfortable for one person working, including video calls.
  • 15 GB a month goes quickly on a laptop. Video calls and streaming use roughly 1 to 3 GB an hour, and one operating system update can take several gigabytes. Working full days on it, expect about a week.
  • 50 GB a month covers ordinary work for most of a month if you keep video quality down and pause cloud sync.

FAQ

Does Visible have unlimited hotspot?

Yes, the amount of hotspot data is unlimited on all three Visible plans. The speed is capped: up to 5 Mbps on Visible, 10 Mbps on Visible+ and 15 Mbps on Visible+ Pro.

Which Cricket plans include hotspot?

Smart Unlimited includes 15 GB a month and Supreme Unlimited includes 50 GB. Select Unlimited does not include hotspot but offers it as a paid add-on. Sensible 10GB has no hotspot option.

Does Google Fi Unlimited Essentials include hotspot?

No. Google's plan page says Unlimited Essentials has no hotspot capability. Unlimited Standard includes 25 GB and Unlimited Premium includes 50 GB.

My prepaid hotspot is unlimited. Why is my laptop slow?

Unlimited often refers to the amount of data and not the speed. Some plans cap hotspot speed at a few Mbps from the first byte, and others slow the hotspot down after a monthly allowance. Run a speed test on the phone and then on the laptop: if only the laptop is slow, it is the hotspot limit.

Does USB tethering get around a prepaid hotspot limit?

No. USB tethering is Personal Hotspot over a cable and is counted and capped the same way as the Wi-Fi hotspot.

The short version

Visible caps hotspot speed and never cuts it off. Cricket, US Mobile and Google Fi give the hotspot a monthly allowance that ranges from 5 GB to 100 GB, and the cheapest tiers at Cricket and Google Fi have no hotspot at all. Read the Broadband Facts label for the plan you are on, because that is the figure that counts.
