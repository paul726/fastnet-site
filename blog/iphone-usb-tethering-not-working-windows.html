iPhone USB Tethering Not Working on Windows 11 or 10? Fix It Step by Step

When USB tethering works, Windows shows a new wired network a few seconds after you plug in the iPhone. When it does not, the cause is nearly always one of four things: Apple's driver is missing, the PC is not trusted, Personal Hotspot is off, or the cable only charges. Here they are in the order worth checking.

What working looks like

Press Win + R, type ncpa.cpl and press Enter. With the iPhone plugged in and sharing, the list has an adapter called Apple Mobile Device Ethernet that shows as connected, and the iPhone has a green indicator at the top of its screen. If that adapter is missing, start at step 1. If it is there but says the cable is unplugged, go to step 3.

The nine checks

1. Check Personal Hotspot on the iPhone

Unlock the phone, open Settings › Personal Hotspot and turn on Allow Others to Join. Cellular data must be on. If Personal Hotspot is missing from Settings or tells you to contact your carrier, your plan does not include hotspot use, and nothing on the PC will change that.

2. Install Apple's driver

Windows has no built-in driver for iPhone tethering. Install Apple Devices or iTunes from the Microsoft Store; either one brings Apple's USB drivers. You do not need to sign in or sync. Restart the PC, then plug the iPhone in again. This is the most common cause on a new PC.

3. Trust the computer

With the phone unlocked, plug it in and tap Trust, then enter your passcode. If the prompt never appears, reset it: Settings › General › Transfer or Reset iPhone › Reset › Reset Location & Privacy, then plug in again. That reset also clears the location and privacy permissions you have given apps, so they will ask again.

4. Change the cable and the port

Many cheap cables carry power only. If the phone charges but Windows makes no sound and shows no device, try the cable that came with the iPhone. Plug it straight into the PC, not into a hub, a dock or a monitor's USB port.

5. Look in Device Manager

Right-click the Start button and open Device Manager. Under Network adapters you want to see Apple Mobile Device Ethernet. If the iPhone shows up only under Portable Devices, or as an item with a yellow warning sign, right-click it and choose Update driver › Search automatically for drivers. Then check Settings › Windows Update › Advanced options › Optional updates › Driver updates for an Apple driver and install it.

6. Enable the adapter

Back in ncpa.cpl, if Apple Mobile Device Ethernet is greyed out, right-click it and choose Enable.

7. Restart Apple's service

This applies if you installed iTunes from Apple's website and not from the Store. Press Win + R, run services.msc, find Apple Mobile Device Service, and choose Restart. Set its startup type to Automatic.

8. Turn it off and on again, in this order

Unplug the phone. Turn Personal Hotspot off. Restart the PC and the iPhone. Plug in, unlock, turn Personal Hotspot back on. It is dull advice, and it clears a stuck driver more often than anything else on this list.

9. Check for software that blocks new network adapters

Some VPN clients and security suites block any network adapter they do not know, and work laptops are often locked down the same way by company policy. Disconnect the VPN and try again. On a managed work laptop the IT department may have to allow it.

Connected, but no internet

  • Open a web page on the iPhone itself. If the phone has no data, the PC will not either.
  • Turn off Wi-Fi on the PC for a moment, so you know which network it is using.
  • Turn Personal Hotspot off and on again while the cable stays plugged in.

Connected, but very slow

USB tethering is Personal Hotspot over a cable, and it draws from the same hotspot allowance as the Wi-Fi hotspot (details here). If a speed test on the phone is fast and the same test on the PC sits near 0.5 Mbps, the allowance has run out and your carrier is slowing tethered devices until the next billing month. FAQ

Do I need iTunes for iPhone USB tethering on Windows?

You need Apple's USB driver, and it comes with either iTunes or the Apple Devices app from the Microsoft Store. You do not have to open the app, sign in or sync anything.

Why does Apple Mobile Device Ethernet say the network cable is unplugged?

The driver is installed but the iPhone is not sharing. Unlock the phone, tap Trust if it asks, and turn on Allow Others to Join under Personal Hotspot.

Why does my iPhone charge but not show up on the PC?

The cable probably carries power only, or the USB port is on a hub that does not pass data. Use the cable that came with the iPhone and plug it directly into the PC.

Does USB tethering charge the iPhone?

Yes. The phone charges from the PC's USB port while it shares its connection, which makes USB a good choice for long sessions.

Does USB tethering work without a hotspot plan?

No. USB tethering is part of Personal Hotspot. If your plan does not include hotspot use, the option is missing or greyed out on the iPhone, and a cable does not get around that.

The short version

Install Apple Devices or iTunes for the driver, unlock the phone and tap Trust, turn on Allow Others to Join, and use a real data cable plugged straight into the PC. That fixes nearly every case. If it connects and is just slow, you have run out of hotspot allowance, which is a different problem.
