PairVPN Not Working, or Missing From the App Store? Where Things Stand

People searching for this have one of two problems. Either the app cannot be found or installed any more, or it is installed and will not connect. The first has a simple cause: PairVPN was removed from the iOS App Store in October 2026. The second can often still be fixed, and the checklist is below.

PairVPN's status

Checked on November 24, 2026:

Nobody outside the company knows whether the removal is permanent. We will update this page if the listing returns.

If you cannot find PairVPN in the App Store

  • Check your purchase history. Open the App Store, tap your picture, then Apps (or Purchased), and search for PairVPN. An app you downloaded before can sometimes be downloaded again after its listing is gone. Whether that works here depends on why it was removed, and we could not confirm it either way.
  • If it is still on your iPhone, protect that copy. Do not delete it. Turn off Settings › App Store › Offload Unused Apps, because an offloaded app has to be downloaded again from the App Store before it will open.
  • Expect to lose it with a new iPhone. When you set up a new phone, apps are downloaded again from the App Store, so an app that is no longer there usually does not come across.
  • Be careful with sideloading. Installing an app file from somewhere else needs a signing tool and regular re-signing. An app of this kind handles all of your computer's traffic, so a modified copy from an unknown source is a real risk.

If it is installed but will not connect

These checks come from PairVPN's own hotspot guide, in the order worth trying:

  1. Start the hotspot first. Turn on Personal Hotspot (or USB tethering), connect the computer, and confirm a web page loads before you start PairVPN on either side.
  2. Check the modes. The phone runs as the server and the computer as the client. Reversed, it will not work.
  3. Keep the iPhone's screen on with PairVPN in front. iOS stops the server soon after the app leaves the screen or the phone locks.
  4. Close other tethering apps. The guide says not to run an app such as PdaNet at the same time.
  5. Connect one device only. PairVPN supported a single client at a time.
  6. Look at the tunnel type. After connecting it should read “Tunnel: local”. If it says remote or relayed, the direct link over the hotspot failed and traffic is not taking the path you want.
  7. Pair again. On the computer choose a new server, and enter the 6-digit code on the phone.

If it stopped working right after an iOS update, there may be nothing to fix on your side. The Android build dates from 2023 and nothing suggests the iPhone app is still maintained, so any iOS release can break it.

FAQ

Why can't I find PairVPN in the App Store?

The iOS listing was removed in October 2026. The App Store page returns “not found” and there has been no announcement from the developer.

Is PairVPN shut down?

There is no official statement. The website is still online and the Android app is still on Google Play, but that build dates from September 2023, the iOS app is gone from the App Store, and the download page for the computer apps is offline.

Will PairVPN come back to the App Store?

We do not know. As of November 24, 2026 nothing has been announced.

Can I move PairVPN to a new iPhone?

Usually not. Apps are downloaded again from the App Store when you set up a new iPhone, and PairVPN is no longer there to download.

Does PairVPN still work if it is already installed?

It can, until an iOS update or a new device breaks it. If it will not connect, start the hotspot first, keep the iPhone's screen on with PairVPN in front, and check that the tunnel reads local.

The short version

PairVPN is gone from the iOS App Store and its other downloads are offline, with no word from the developer. A copy you already have may keep working: do not delete it, and turn off app offloading. When it stops, FastNet Connect + ProxyDeck covers the Mac or Windows PC through an iPhone case.
